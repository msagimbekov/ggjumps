## Full description

One bubble. 100 islands. 99 jumps.

99 jumps is a relaxing one-finger arcade game about a liquid bubble hopping from island to island across a calm tropical sea. Easy to pick up, tricky to master: aim with a slingshot, judge the distance, and see how far you can get before the water wins.

How to play:
Drag back from the bubble to aim, then let go to jump. The further you pull, the further it flies. Land on the next island and keep going, or splash into the sea and lose a life. You start with three lives.

The islands don't make it easy:
• Grass stops you where you land
• Ice keeps you sliding
• Sandbanks drift from side to side while you aim

Pick up what you find on the way:
• Bubble drops: collect three and they turn into an extra life
• Golden spyglass: shows exactly where your jump will land, for a few seconds

Reach the last island and the fireworks go off.

Race the clock:
• Your time runs from the first jump to the last island
• Put your name on the global leaderboard and try to beat the fastest players
• Share your finish time with friends

Also:
• Pick your bubble colour: Lagoon, Seafoam or Coral
• Calm beach music and soft water sounds, perfect for a short break
• Quick "How to play" cards for new players, always available in Settings
• Rate the game, share it or send us feedback right from Settings
• Double-tap the water and see what happens

A casual skill game for anyone who likes jump games, slingshot aiming and island hopping. Easy to learn, hard to finish. How fast can you cross all 100?
