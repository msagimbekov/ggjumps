## App name

99 jumps

## Short description

Drag, aim and jump a liquid bubble across 100 islands. Don't fall in!

## Full description

One bubble. 100 islands. 99 jumps.

Drag back from the bubble to aim, then let go to jump. The further you pull, the further it flies. Land on the next island and keep going, or splash into the sea and lose a life.

The islands don't make it easy:
• Grass stops you where you land
• Ice keeps you sliding
• Sandbanks drift from side to side while you aim

Pick up what you find on the way:
• Bubble drops: collect three and they turn into an extra life
• Golden spyglass: shows exactly where your jump will land, for a few seconds

Reach the last island and the fireworks go off. Put your time on the leaderboard and share it with your friends.

Also:
• Pick your bubble colour: Lagoon, Seafoam or Coral
• Calm beach music and soft water sounds
• Double-tap the water and see what happens

Easy to learn, hard to finish. How fast can you cross all 100?
