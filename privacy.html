Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

99 jumps ("the game") is made by Madi Sagimbekov. This page explains what the game stores and sends. There is no account, no sign-in and no tracking of who you are.

On your phone

Your settings (volume, bubble colour) and whether you have seen the "How to play" cards are saved on your phone only.

Leaderboard (only if you join)

If you make it across all the islands you can type a name. The name, your run time and the date are stored on our server (Supabase, hosted in the EU) and shown to every player on the leaderboard. Do not use your real name if you do not want it shown. To have an entry removed, ask through the support page.

Game updates

The game checks our website for small game updates and downloads them. This sends no personal information.

Ads

When you run out of lives you can choose to watch a rewarded video ad for one more life, served by Google AdMob. Ads only play when you ask for one. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier, approximate location and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. In the EU and UK the game first shows Google's consent form, where you choose what is allowed; you can change your choice later under Settings → Privacy options. You can also limit ad personalisation in your phone's own ad settings. See Google's advertising policy.

Sharing

"Share my time" makes a picture on your phone and hands it to your phone's own share menu. The game does not see where you share it.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold, or ask us to delete a leaderboard entry, through the support page. Deleting the game removes everything stored on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
