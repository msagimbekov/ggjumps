99 jumps

One bubble. 100 islands. 99 jumps.

99 jumps is a casual game: drag back from a liquid bubble to aim, let go to jump, and hop from island to island across the sea. Grass, ice and drifting sandbanks, bubble drops for extra lives and a golden spyglass that shows where you will land.
