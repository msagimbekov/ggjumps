99 jumps

One bubble. 100 islands. 99 jumps.

99 jumps is a casual game: drag back from a liquid bubble to aim, let go to jump, and hop from island to island across the sea. Grass, ice and drifting sandbanks, bubble drops for extra lives and a golden spyglass that shows where you will land.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue and describe what happened, your phone and the game version: github.com/msagimbekov/ggjumps/issues

Delete your data

The only data 99 jumps keeps off your phone is a leaderboard entry (the name you typed, your run time and the date), and only if you added one. To have it deleted, open an issue at github.com/msagimbekov/ggjumps/issues titled "Delete my leaderboard entry" with the name and the time shown on the leaderboard. It is removed within 30 days and nothing is kept. Everything else is stored on your phone only and goes away when you delete the game. Ad data is handled by Google; see Google's advertising policy.
